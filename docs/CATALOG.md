# Course catalogue

[Start](../README.md) · [Assessment](ASSESSMENT.md) · [Learning paths](LEARNING_PATHS.md) · [Original courses](RESEARCH.md#original-link-audit)

Reviewed **2026-10-26**. Resource links are primary course or author sources. Selection favours, in order: authority (universities, public learning programmes, standards bodies, and the primary tool vendor's own training), checkable positive reception (ratings, enrolment, adoption in other courses), and genuinely free access to the material and practice. The courses were not completed, lab environments were not run, and account-specific entitlements were not tested. [Research notes](RESEARCH.md#curriculum-review-2026-10-26) record the evidence; [verification limits](RESEARCH.md#verification-limits) identify blocked pages and index-only evidence. Ratings and counts are rounded snapshots from the review date.

## Reading the entries

- **Open:** publicly readable/watchable learning material; registration may be needed for progress, submission, or a certificate.
- **Video tier:** free videos advertised; labs, assessments, or certificates may require payment. Recheck before enrolling.
- **Platform:** listing available; full free access unconfirmed or explicitly unavailable. A free sign-up or trial does not establish a free complete course.
- **Runtime extra:** model/API usage, hosted labs, cloud resources, or GPU compute can cost money even when teaching material is open.
- **Free certificate:** stated by the provider; conditions vary. A certificate is never required by a path exit check and is never the reason a resource was chosen.

Suggested scopes and hours are **our estimates for selected study blocks**, including some practice, not provider estimates for completing entire courses. All entries are English unless stated otherwise. `C` identifies courses; `R` identifies readings, books, datasets, or documentation. IDs are never renumbered or reused.

## Choosing without overspending

Every path has an open route: C01 or C34 (P0); C02, C04, R13 (P1); C38 and R12 (W1); C31 watched without solving, plus R15 (W2); R16 and C40 (W3); C04, R18, and the paper fallback (W4); C28, R11, R19 (D1); R19, R20, C18 (D2); C11, C44, R17, R09 (D3); C06, C07, R21, R22 (D4); C54 and R23 (T1). The practice briefs also work manually or with simulated tool responses. Live agents require a permitted tool and a usage budget. Certificates, model usage, hardware, and corporate entitlements are separate decisions.

Several open routes offer a free certificate or badge (for example C01, C02, C11, C34, C38), and C01, C05, and C36 offer university credit through Helsinki Open University. Path exit checks never require them.

The [original-link audit](RESEARCH.md#original-link-audit) preserves business, HR, sales, marketing, leadership, Scala, cloud, statistics, and well-being electives. Their placement outside the default paths is a scope decision, not a negative course review. C17 (Full Stack LLM Bootcamp, April 2023 recordings) was retired on 2026-10-26; its ID is not reused.
